Test your own deck

You have a deck and want to know how strong it really is?

  • 120 games against 20 ranked decks, all in the same budget
  • Elo, tier and your approximate place in the rankings
  • Tips backed by the games and swap ideas within your budget
  • A short email, all details on your private page

A Moxfield or Archidekt link or the list as text. A test usually takes three to four hours. How it works · See an example result

Looking for a deck for a specific commander and your budget?

  • We build it from the EDHREC lists, never over your budget
  • Tested in 120 games, just like your own deck

Test your playgroup

Want to know how balanced your playgroup is?

  • 3 or 4 decks from your group, 120 games only against each other
  • Win rate of each deck – and whether your group is balanced
  • Who knocks out whom, placement and the edge of going first
  • The full analysis with tips for every deck

Free like the other tests while there are free slots. Moxfield or Archidekt links or the lists as text. How it works · See an example result
