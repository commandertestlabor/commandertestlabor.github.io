Which cards are in decks that win often? 82 cards from 448 tested decks.

How to read the numbers

Win rate: how often the tested decks that play this card win. Four players sit at the table – 25% is normal. Green = more, red = less.

Tier S–D: rank among all cards here – S is the top 10%, A to 30%, B the midfield, C to 90%, D the rest.

Popularity: how many of our tested decks play the card.

Only cards from the decks of at least 8 different commanders are shown – each commander counts once. A card does not win alone: the number shows whether it is often found in winning decks.
