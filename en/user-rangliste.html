Decks with the same number of wins share a place. The tier comes from the wins: 31+ S, 17+ A, 10+ B, 6+ C, below that D. Only each player's best deck per class counts.

How it works

  1. Pick a classUp to €100, €200 or €500 – you pick the class you enter. Your deck can't cost more than that on Cardmarket.
  2. Play 60 gamesYour deck plays 60 games in Forge against 30 decks from every tier. Everyone in your class gets the same opponents this season – that keeps it fair.
  3. Collect winsWhat counts is how often you win. Your best deck counts in every class. You get your result and your place by email.

Supporters can send 5 decks a week, everyone else one – as long as free slots are open. Only your player name is shown, never your email. Every new name gets a quick check.

When does the season change? As soon as a new Magic set is out and our Forge knows it. Then there are new opponents, the new cards are allowed, and the old season moves to the archive with its winners.
