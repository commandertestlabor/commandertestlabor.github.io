Strengths

  • +Uses its mana well: only 17% of lands went unused in a turn (all-deck avg: 25%) – the deck almost always has something to play logged (measured on own turns 3–8, 70 games)

Weaknesses

  • –Depends on its commander: only 0% wins without it, 30% with it indication (29 games without, 172 with commander)

  • –Weak against board wipes: 17% wins when opponents cast a board wipe, 40% without one (all-deck avg: 23% vs 29%) indication (129 / 72 games)
