Strengths and weaknesses

From 50 analysed games in 5 tournaments (tournament lists as played by Forge). Games that hit the time limit don't count, just like in the rankings. Values in brackets: average of all decks in the same price class.

Play style: wins through combat – 89% of the damage to opponents is combat damage · How it wins: 79% of wins by surviving (knocked out at most one opponent itself, the others took each other out) – all-deck avg: 36%; 4 wins via a win-the-game card (Test of Endurance 2×, Felidar Sovereign 2×)

Strengths

  • +Uses its mana well: only 15% of lands went unused in a turn (all-deck avg: 26%) – the deck almost always has something to play logged (measured on own turns 3–8, 50 games)
  • +Often wins by combo: in 9 of 19 wins all pieces of a combo were in play indication
  • +Rarely out first: eliminated first in only 14% of games (all-deck avg: 25%) logged (7 of 50 games)
  • +Wins without its commander too: 37% wins in games where it never came down logged (19 games without, 31 with commander)

Weaknesses

  • –Vulnerable to direct damage: only 23% of losses to combat, the rest to spells, drain or abilities (all-deck avg: 62% combat) logged (7 of 31 losses)
  • –Little pressure: only avg 16 damage to opponents per game (all-deck avg: 29) logged (50 games)
  • –Rarely eliminates opponents: 0.44 per game on average (all-deck avg: 0.62) logged (50 games)
