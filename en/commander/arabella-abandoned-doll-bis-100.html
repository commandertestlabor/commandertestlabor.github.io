This commander dropped out of the EDHREC top, so it no longer plays in new tournaments. All numbers here come from its 216 games so far. It is no longer listed in the rankings.

Time limit note: 6% of this deck's games hit the time limit without a winner even on the third try (all decks in this price class: 4%). Usually the Forge AI is stuck in a loop then, e.g. with lots of tokens. These games don't count – so the win rate is somewhat less meaningful.

At most €100 including the commander. If prices rise above that, the shopping list is rebuilt automatically.
