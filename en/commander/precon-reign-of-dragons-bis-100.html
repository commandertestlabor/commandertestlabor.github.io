Official Commander precon “Reign of Dragons” – played exactly as sold in stores, without a single change. It plays in every tournament and price class as a benchmark, so it is not ranked. Why do precons play?

Strengths and weaknesses

From 130 analysed games in 13 tournaments (tournament lists as played by Forge). Games that hit the time limit don't count, just like in the rankings. Values in brackets: average of all decks in the same price class.

Play style: wins through combat – 79% of the damage to opponents is combat damage · How it wins: 24% of wins by surviving (knocked out at most one opponent itself, the others took each other out) – all-deck avg: 31%

Strengths

  • +Uses its mana well: only 13% of lands went unused in a turn (all-deck avg: 26%) – the deck almost always has something to play logged (measured on own turns 3–8, 130 games)
  • +Applies a lot of pressure: avg 42 damage to opponents per game (all-deck avg: 31) logged (130 games)
  • +Attacks get through: 11% of attackers blocked (all-deck avg: 19%) logged (1031 attackers in 130 games)
  • +Clears the table: eliminates 0.83 opponents per game on average (all-deck avg: 0.63) logged (130 games)

Weaknesses

  • –Depends on its commander: only 0% wins without it, 35% with it indication (12 games without, 118 with commander)
  • –Drops out early: in losses on average already on its 9th own turn (all-deck avg: 10th) logged (89 losses)
