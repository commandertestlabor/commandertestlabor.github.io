What the deck could work on

Only clear differences to the opponents in the same games, each with evidence – most important first.

  1. Defense: you were the first one out in 25 of 60 games (about 15 would be expected). More blockers, cheap removal or life gain keep you in the game longer.
  2. Pressure: you deal 7 damage per game on average, your opponents 32. More attackers or a clear finisher help close out games.
  3. Against strong decks (tier S/A) you win 9%, against weaker ones (C/D) 29%. More interaction – removal, counterspells or a board wipe against their key cards – is most likely to close the gap.
